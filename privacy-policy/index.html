Privacy Policy

Updated 23 July 2026 · Fotile Pakistan

Fotile Pakistan respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

1Information We Collect

We collect information you provide and information gathered automatically as you browse:

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email, delivery address and order details when you place an order, request a quote, or contact us.
  • Payment information: when you pay by card or wallet, your payment details are entered on and processed by our secure payment gateway. Fotile does not collect or store your full card number, CVV or PIN on our own servers.
  • Automatic information: device type, browser, pages viewed, and general location, collected through cookies and analytics tools to improve the site.

2How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and arrange installation and after-sales service.
  • To respond to your enquiries, quotes and support requests.
  • To improve our website, products and customer experience.
  • To send order updates and, where you have agreed, offers and news. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

3Payment Security

Card and wallet payments are handled by a State Bank of Pakistan-approved payment gateway over an encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant connection. Sensitive card data is transmitted directly to the gateway and is never stored by Fotile. Our website uses SSL encryption to protect information in transit.

4How We Store & Protect Data

We keep your personal information only for as long as needed to fulfil orders, provide warranty and service, and meet legal requirements. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

5Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary with:

  • Courier and logistics partners, to deliver your order.
  • Payment gateways and banks, to process your payment.
  • Authorised dealers and service partners, to provide installation and after-sales support.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

6Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

7Your Choices & Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • You may unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time.
  • To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a revised date.

9Contact Us

For any privacy question or request, contact Fotile Pakistan at 042-111-131-517 or via our Contact page.
