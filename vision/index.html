Our Vision

Kitchens that make families happier.

Since 1996, Fotile has had one obsession: making the kitchen a healthier, cleaner and more joyful place to be.

Our mission

The kitchen is where care begins.

We believe the kitchen is the heart of the home — where health begins, where families gather, and where everyday care is expressed through cooking.

Our job is to remove the smoke, the noise and the effort that get in the way, so cooking becomes something you look forward to.

Health-first engineering

Every product starts with one question: is the kitchen healthier?

Clean air

Range hoods that capture smoke at the source

Side-draft suction pulls smoke and grease away before it reaches you — or the rest of your home.
Clean food

A sink that washes dishes, fruit and vegetables

The in-sink dishwasher saves time and water, and cleans produce as well as plates.
Better cooking

Ovens and hobs built for control

Steam, bake and precise flame control for lighter, healthier everyday cooking.
Our journey

Three decades, one kitchen at a time.

  1. 1996

    Fotile is founded

    Fotile is born in Ningbo, China, with a single product: a range hood.

  2. 1999

    Quality over price

    During an industry price war, Fotile refuses to cut corners — and launches a better hood instead.

  3. 2002

    Fotile comes to Pakistan

    Pakistan becomes one of Fotile’s first international markets.

  4. 2015

    The in-sink dishwasher

    Fotile launches a world-first: a sink, dishwasher and fruit-and-vegetable cleaner in one.

  5. Today

    200 million homes

    Fotile appliances are used in over 200 million homes across 50+ countries.

  6. 2026

    The F20 refrigerator

    Fotile brings its built-in F20 refrigerator to Pakistani kitchens. Discover the F20 →
