Designed in Berlin

Art, engineered into every detail.

The F20 was shaped by EMAMI Design Studio in Berlin — the studio behind the BMW MINI Cooper — with founder Arman Emami leading the work himself. Fotile invested over 10 million RMB in its design alone.

  • Seamless, built-in look — no visible handles, no gaps; it blends into your cabinetry.
  • Press to open — touch anywhere on the door and it opens; leave it and it gently closes itself.
  • Apple-Store brushed steel — Fotile is the first appliance brand to use this finish.
  • Sandblasted stainless steel — high-quality compartment back panel and inner door material, with adjustable shelves and ambient lighting.
