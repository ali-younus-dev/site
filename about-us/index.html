Who We Are

For more than 20 years, Fotile has served the Pakistani customer — building a reputation for kitchen appliances that are functional and stylish.

Fotile is the leading kitchen appliances brand in Pakistan. From kitchenware to small appliances, we offer a wide range of products to meet the needs of every kitchen.

As Asia's leading kitchen appliances brand, our products are designed to make your life in the kitchen easier and more enjoyable — guaranteeing a fresh, smoke-free and healthier home.

"We have quickly risen to prominence as a leader in both culinary innovation and improving lifestyles — growing from small beginnings to a global presence."
