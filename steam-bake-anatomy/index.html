Humidity control

Crisp outside.
Tender inside.

A wax-motor mechanism opens and closes an exhaust to manage humidity inside the cavity — venting moisture promptly for crisp results, or holding it in for juicy ones. It works in Bake, Air Fry and Dehydrate.

Low humidity

Crisp, aromatic, fatty results — crispy-skin meat, chicken wings, croissants.

Medium humidity

Tender and juicy where food would dry out — skewers, chicken breast, baked fish.

High humidity

Soft centres and a good rise — artisan bread, whole chicken, thick steak.

03 — Two zones

Two temperatures.
One oven.

Top and bottom heat are controlled independently, with up to a 70 °C difference. Cook two different dishes at once, or give one dish a crisp top and a gentle base.

Steam & bake together

Roast on top.
Steam below.

The heating elements and the steam generator run separately. A steam-bake tray splits the cavity: elements bake the upper zone while powerful steam cooks the lower zone — two dishes from a single operation, ready at the same time.
