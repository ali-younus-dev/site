Quick answers

Dealer FAQs.

How many Fotile dealers are there in Pakistan?

Fotile Pakistan has more than 200 authorised dealers across the country, along with its own showrooms in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Multan.

How do I find the Fotile dealer nearest to me?

Call the Fotile helpline on 042-111-13-15-17 and the team will point you to the authorised dealer closest to you. You can also walk into any Fotile showroom.

Why should I buy from an authorised Fotile dealer?

Authorised dealers sell genuine Fotile appliances that carry the manufacturer's warranty, and they can arrange Fotile-authorised installation and after-sales service.

Where can I see Fotile appliances before buying?

Visit a Fotile showroom to see the range hoods, hobs, ovens, dishwashers and the F20 refrigerator in person. Addresses and directions are on the Showrooms page.
