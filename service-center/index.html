Quick answers

Helpline FAQs.

What is the Fotile helpline number in Pakistan?

The official Fotile Pakistan helpline (UAN) is 042-111-13-15-17. Call for service bookings, complaints, spare parts and any product questions, nationwide.

How do I book a Fotile service or repair?

Call 042-111-13-15-17, or book online on the Fotile Service Center portal at fotileservicecenter.com. Choose your appliance, describe the issue, pin your location and our team calls you back to confirm a technician visit.

Which appliances does Fotile service cover?

All Fotile products: range hoods, hobs, ovens, microwaves, dishwashers, water purifiers and the F20 refrigerator, for repair, installation and maintenance using genuine Fotile parts.

Where is Fotile service available in Pakistan?

Fotile technicians operate nationwide, with fast coverage in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and other major cities. Book by phone or online and the nearest team is dispatched.
