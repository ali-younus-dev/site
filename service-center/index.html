Helpline FAQs.

What is the Fotile helpline number in Pakistan?+
The official Fotile Pakistan helpline (UAN) is 042-111-131-517 — for service bookings, complaints, spare parts and product questions, nationwide.
How do I book a Fotile service or repair?+
Two ways: call 042-111-131-517, or book online at fotileservicecenter.com — pick your appliance, describe the issue, pin your location, and we call you back to confirm.
Which appliances are covered?+
Every Fotile product — range hoods, hobs, ovens, microwaves, dishwashers, water purifiers, and soon the F20 refrigerator — for repair, installation and maintenance with genuine parts.
Where is service available?+
Across Pakistan — fastest in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad, with nationwide dispatch for other cities.
