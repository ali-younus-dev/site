01 — Capture

Catch it at the pan.
Clear it from the air.

Three jobs, done in order: gather the smoke, pull it in fast, and send it out cleanly through a powerful motor and a smooth duct.

STAGE 1

Strong capture

An M-shaped smoke chamber gathers rising smoke before it can spread.

STAGE 2

Fast suction

Full-coverage smoke inlets draw air in across the whole width of the hood.

STAGE 3

Clean exhaust

A powerful motor and a smooth duct path send smoke out cleanly.
