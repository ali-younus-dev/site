The Fotile Range Hood EMS9028s is a 90cm-wide stainless steel chimney hood with a black panel surface, certified to CB and SIRIM. Powered by a 270W AC motor, it delivers up to 1500 m³/hr airflow with a 92% oil filtration rate and 97% odor reduction, and features Touch and Wave control at a quiet ≤55 dB.
