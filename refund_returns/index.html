Refund & Return Policy

Updated 23 July 2026 · Fotile Pakistan

At Fotile Pakistan, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains when and how you can return a product or request a refund. It applies to purchases made through this website and our authorised channels.

1Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order, provided the product meets the eligibility conditions below. For manufacturing faults covered by warranty, our standard warranty terms apply beyond this window (see Warranty below).

2Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, the product must be:

  • Unused, uninstalled and in its original condition.
  • In its original packaging, with all accessories, manuals and free items included.
  • Accompanied by the original invoice or proof of purchase.

Products that cannot be returned

  • Items that have been installed, used or damaged after delivery.
  • Products with missing packaging, accessories or serial numbers.
  • Items marked as clearance, final sale or made-to-order.

3Damaged, Defective or Wrong Items

If your product arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order number. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund at no cost to you. Please inspect your order on delivery before signing for it where possible.

4How to Request a Return

  • Contact our team at 042-111-131-517 or through the Contact page with your order number and reason.
  • Our team will confirm eligibility and share return or pickup instructions.
  • Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the outcome.

5Refunds

Once a return is approved and the product has been inspected, your refund will be processed as follows:

  • Card & online payments: refunded to the original payment method, typically within 7–10 business days after approval. The exact timing depends on your bank or payment provider.
  • Bank transfer: refunded to the bank account you provide, usually within 3–7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery: refunded by bank transfer to your provided account details.

Any payment-gateway or transaction charges, and delivery charges on non-faulty returns, may be deducted from the refund where applicable. Refunds are issued for the amount actually paid.

6Order Cancellations

You may cancel an order before it has been dispatched for a full refund. Once an order has been shipped, it will be handled under the returns process above. To cancel, contact us as soon as possible with your order number.

7Warranty

Fotile appliances are covered by a manufacturer's warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty claims are handled through our authorised service network. Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, incorrect installation, or normal wear. Keep your invoice as proof of purchase for any warranty claim.

8Contact

For any return, refund or warranty request, contact Fotile Pakistan at 042-111-131-517 or via our Contact page. We're here to help.
