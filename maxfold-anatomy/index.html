01 — The fold

Hidden when idle.
Lowered when it matters.

Most "hidden" hoods hide so well they forget to extract. MaxFold keeps its 330 mm body fixed inside the cabinet and folds only the intake down — closer to the fumes, then back into a seamless flat line.

330mmBody depth — 40% slimmer than European-style hoods; fits a 350 mm cabinet.
10cmThe intake drops 10 cm; with the oil cup the lowest point sits 14 cm below the body.
135°Auto fume-screen opens wide to catch more of the plume.
15yearsAviation-grade folding structure, passed 50,000-cycle life testing.

Inspired by aircraft landing-gear structure. Dimensions from Fotile's MaxFold brochure and training material.

Flat when closed · one touch to open
02 — The catch

Reach further.
Lose less smoke.

Fume escapes from the front and the edges of a pan. MaxFold lowers its intake, opens a 135° screen and pulls air straight through a rear intake — so the capture zone reaches 413 mm out from the wall instead of the typical 280 mm.

06 — The senses

It notices.
So you don't have to.

WAVE CONTROL

Wave, it wakes

Wave past the sensing windows: the fume screen opens, the fan runs on Auto and the light comes on. No touching a greasy panel.

SMART ODOR SENSING

It smells the air

A 7×24-hour sensor watches for formaldehyde, ammonia, CO, benzene, smoke and more — and clears the air within 60 seconds.

HOOD–HOB LINK

Flame on, hood on

Pair the hood with a compatible Fotile hob: the hood starts when you light the burner and clears the air when you switch it off.

2-MINUTE DELAY

Cleans up after itself

When you finish cooking it keeps running for two more minutes to pull the last of the smoke, then switches itself off.
